# Changelog

## v1.3: a gift from the sky
- After all twelve constellations, the signs light up one by one with a soft rising chime, then the moon slowly fills into a full moon
- The moon always shows at least a thin crescent and keeps its soft halo, so the sky stays bright on new-moon nights (the label still shows the real phase)
- The moon now fades away completely at sunrise
- Removed the aurora: it made the sky feel crowded instead of calm

## v1.2: small comforts
- Soft music-box chime on every caught star, generated from sine waves (pentatonic, so notes never clash)
- The moon now matches tonight's real phase, with a label underneath
- Breathing guide on **B**: breathe in for 4 s, out for 6 s
- Aurora once all twelve constellations are revealed
- **M** now mutes all sound (music and chimes)

## v1.1: sunrise
- Leaving (Esc or closing the window) starts an 8-second sunrise instead of quitting instantly
- Dawn sky, sun rising behind two layers of hills, pink-gold clouds, a pale morning moon and a small flock of birds
- Music fades out with the sunrise; press Esc again to skip

## v1.0: the night
- Realistic night sky: gradient, Milky Way, dim and twinkling stars, drifting clouds, moon with a breathing halo
- Falling stars with fading tails; catching one releases a ring of light and floating glitter
- Missed stars sink softly into the horizon, with occasional kind messages
- Twelve zodiac constellations that trace themselves every 8 wishes, starting with Pisces
- Optional looping background music, pause and mute
